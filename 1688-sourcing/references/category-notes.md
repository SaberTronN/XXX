# Category notes: cookware (锅具)

Use these as a starting point. Check against what you actually see on 1688.

## Detachable-handle / stackable cookware

Chinese terms to search:
- 可拆卸手柄锅具套装, 可拆卸手柄锅, 可拆把手锅具, 活动手柄锅, 可拆手柄不粘锅
- 叠叠锅, 叠叠乐锅具, 收纳锅具套装, 可叠放锅具, 节省空间锅具
- 一柄多锅, 无柄锅 套装, 韩式可拆卸锅具
- Add material / coating: 压铸铝, 锻打铝, 不锈钢, 三层钢 / 复合底, 陶瓷涂层, 麦饭石,
  石纹, 不含氟 / 无氟 / 无涂层
- Add positioning: 源头工厂, 跨境, 外贸出口, OEM, 定制logo, 亚马逊

Defining features: one removable handle shared across pots/pans; pots nest for storage;
usually oven-safe and fridge-storable without the handle.

Typical variants: 4–17 piece sets; die-cast aluminium with nonstick (most common, cheapest),
forged aluminium, stainless tri-ply (premium), ceramic-coated; cream, beige, green, grey,
black, pink colourways.

## Main cookware clusters

- **永康, 浙江 (Yongkang, Zhejiang)** — China's cookware capital. Most die-cast/forged
  aluminium nonstick factories; many detachable-handle makers.
- **武义, 浙江 (Wuyi, Zhejiang)** — next to Yongkang; cookware and kitchenware.
- **金华 / 缙云, 浙江** — surrounding area, more aluminium cookware plants.
- **潮州 / 潮安 彩塘, 广东 (Chaozhou, Guangdong)** — stainless steel cookware hub.
- **揭阳, 广东 (Jieyang)** — stainless steel kitchenware.
- **阳江, 广东 (Yangjiang)** — knives and stainless kitchenware.
- **宁波, 浙江 (Ningbo)** — export-oriented kitchenware makers and traders.

## Quality and compliance checklist

- Body: die-cast aluminium (thick, heavy, good heat) > forged > pressed/stamped aluminium.
  Iron/carbon steel bodies are cheaper; flag if marketed as aluminium.
- Coating: ask for the brand (Whitford, ILAG, Daikin, Greblon = better; "water-based
  nonstick" alone is generic). Note PTFE vs ceramic vs "fluorine-free" claims.
- Induction: needs a steel plate (often 410 stainless) bonded on the base.
- Handle: check the lock mechanism, max load and whether it fits every piece in the set.
- Food-contact tests: EU → LFGB / EU 1935/2004; US → FDA 21 CFR, Prop 65; also PFOA-free
  reports. Check that the report covers this product and names the factory.
- Common complaints to look for in reviews: coating peeling, scratches, handle wobble or
  slipping, dented rims, warped bases, poor packaging and shipping damage.
